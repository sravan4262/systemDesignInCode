# Leveling and technical-leadership signals

What separates mid-level, senior and staff, so your answers sound like the level you're applying for. Back to [001-start-here](001-start-here.md). Answers question F5 in [002-growth-mistakes](002-growth-mistakes.md).

## The three levels in one table
| | Mid-level | Senior | Staff |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | A feature or a task | A system or a project, end to end | Several teams or a whole area |
| **Autonomy** | Given a clear problem | Given a vague problem; turns it into a plan | Finds the problems nobody assigned |
| **Influence** | Their own code | Their team: reviews, standards, mentoring | Other teams: direction, shared standards |
| **Ambiguity** | Asks for clarity | Creates clarity for the team | Creates clarity for the org |
| **Typical story** | "I built X" | "I led X, chose Y over Z because…, and it cut A by B%" | "I saw teams kept hitting X, proposed Y, and got 4 teams to adopt it" |

## Leadership signals interviewers listen for
For each one, have one example (a story id) ready.

| Signal | What it sounds like | My example |
|---|---|---|
| Writing design docs | "I wrote a one-page design with options and trade-offs, then ran the review" | ❓ |
| Prioritising tech debt | "I put a cost on the debt (incidents, slow deploys) and traded it against features" | ❓ (ST18?) |
| Build vs. buy | "I compared building, the vendor and doing nothing, on cost, risk and time" | ST4 (RegScale) |
| Estimating a project | "I broke it down, added a buffer for unknowns, and said what would change the estimate" | ❓ |
| Raising failure modes before anyone asks | "Before launch I asked what happens if the sync falls behind, and we added an alert" | ❓ (ST8?) |
| Raising the bar | Standards, reviews, automation | ST11 |
| Growing others | Mentoring with a concrete result | ST11 |

## Quick check (answer in your own words, then we grade)
1. In one sentence each: what's the difference between senior and staff?
2. Pick one of your stories and say what would make it sound staff-level instead of senior.
