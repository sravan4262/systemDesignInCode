### ST25 · Two deadlines, one person: user-facing VDA launch feature vs. FedRAMP COGS reduction (Citrix)
- **Answers:** C3 (also managing up, saying no)
- **Status:** first draft in my words, saved as is (2026-11-24).
- **My answer (my words):**
  > There was a time when different work items had conflicting priorities: one had user impact, the other had COGS impact. The user-impact one was a feature to help users with Citrix Workspace during the launch of the VDA. The COGS one was reducing the cost of operating the FedRAMP environment. Both were high priority with deadlines approaching.
  >
  > I raised it with my manager: given the two deadlines, I couldn't pick one, and realistically only one could be finished within its deadline. I said that if both needed to be met, we'd need to find someone who could take on one of them (I was comfortable with either), and check that person was comfortable meeting the deadline, or else find someone else. Or we could adjust the deadline on one of them.
  >
  > My manager scheduled a meeting with the directors. I shared why both couldn't be met, the limitations and difficulties, and the options: assign one to someone else, or re-adjust the priority. The COGS one was de-prioritised, which gave me time to work on both and complete them.
- **Grade:** 7/10 (2026-11-24). Strong: raised the conflict early instead of silently trying both, came with **options** (reassign / move a deadline), not just a problem, and the decision went to the people who owned the trade-off (directors).
- **To strengthen (❓):**
  - **Your recommendation:** you said "I can't pick one". Seniors are expected to recommend: e.g. "the VDA launch is a fixed, external, customer-facing date; COGS savings can slip a few weeks at a known cost". Then let the directors decide.
  - **Criteria, out loud:** user impact at launch vs. $ per month of delay; fixed vs. movable date; who is blocked.
  - **Numbers:** the deadlines, the COGS saving (per month), how long the COGS work slipped.
  - **The ending:** "gave me time to work on both and complete" is vague: say the feature shipped for the launch, and the COGS work shipped N weeks later with the saving.
  - **Communication:** how the COGS stakeholders were told and how they took it.
- **Follow-ups to prep:** Which would you have picked, and why? What did the delay cost? What if the directors had said "do both"?
